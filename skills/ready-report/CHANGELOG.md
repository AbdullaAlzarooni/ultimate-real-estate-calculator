# Changelog — Ready Property Report

## 1.0.0 — 12 Oct 2026
First release, as part of **The Ultimate Real Estate Calculator** v1.3.
- Report for **ready units on the resale market**: verdict (priced above / below recent sales, net rent and yield
  on your cash, sale after N years), four deal checks and a "where the price sits" bar.
- Net yield labels as in the Off-plan Report: **Good ≥ 6% · OK 5–6% · Low < 5%**, with the price that would reach 6%.
- **Cash or mortgage:** UAE National / UAE Resident / Non-resident, 1st or 2nd mortgage (Central Bank loan limits,
  lower above AED 5M), fixed rate for 1–5 years then EIBOR (1M / 3M / 6M / 1Y, that day's Central Bank fixing) +
  bank margin (default 1.50%, editable), monthly payment before and after the fixed period, life and property
  insurance, bank / registration fees, early settlement fee (1%, max AED 10,000) and release fee.
- **Unfurnished and furnished rent** (measured furnished premium; furniture valued at what it earns in 3 years,
  capped at its cost); the default view follows the unit itself.
- **DXB Interact, size-matched** (±10% of the unit's size, custom window that reaches 5 deals, same window for rent)
  as the main source; Bayut second (medians from its own deal list). Each source lists its actual deals.
- **Sell after 1–10 years:** market growth after the growth bands; from year 6 growth is half the rate, max 3% a
  year (falls stay as they are); **building-age discount** −1.5% a year until the building is 6, then −3% a year.
  Every hold card shows From the sale · From rent · Grand total, with the loan still owed at the sale.
- **Supply & demand** from DXB Interact (supply Active + Finished, handovers by DLD year, what buyers bought, the
  building's unit mix).
- **Developer section (optional):** every project by the developer, with photos, DLD dates, % built, unit mix,
  rented %, absorption, price / rent / off-plan premium per unit type, area dropdown, type chips and search.
- View map, location comfort cards, step-by-step calculations (sheet rows in brackets), photo gallery,
  contact footer, client PDF. **Every image must load** before delivery.
- Formulas checked against the author's Ready Property Evaluation Form.
- Example data: Binghatti Azure furnished studio, JVC (with photos and the Binghatti developer section).
- Never uses Property Monitor (it does not allow AI use).
